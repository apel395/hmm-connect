# Customer invoices, AR and payments

`INVOICE` is a customer bill. Posting requires an active customer, due date and Piutang Usaha control account (explicit choice / configured AR account / existing 100-200-101). It creates one uniquely linked UNPAID `AccountReceivable`: debit AR for the full invoice, credit the selected revenue and the saved PBJT liability amounts. It creates no cash/bank receipt or inventory entry. Payment fields submitted by old clients do not mark an invoice paid.

Retail `POS` continues to represent a paid checkout. Invoice mode has customer, due-date and AR controls instead of payment inputs. Draft invoices can be completed in the Sales detail dialog. Posted invoices link to their AR document; its source lines supply the printable standard/PHR invoice details, preserving client layouts. AR summaries, detail, print, aging and customer receipts enforce location, business unit and department access; dimensionless legacy records remain consolidated-only.

On the AR invoice, Receive Payment posts a separate customer receipt: debit the explicitly chosen cash/bank account and credit the invoice's snapshotted AR account. Partial receipts are supported, overpayments refused, and the invoice closes only when outstanding reaches zero. A row lock and unique request key make retries idempotent. Source revenue and PBJT are not reposted. The form recovers unsaved input and its retry key after closing/refreshing. Linked AR reconciliation uses dated invoices and customer receipts; historical dimensionless legacy AR still reports unavailable history rather than invented balances.

Invoices posted by the previous incorrect implementation are not silently rewritten. Finance opens the existing Sales document, selects the real customer and due date, confirms it remains unpaid and uses Correct unpaid invoice to AR. The operation verifies the original posted source journal and matching GL, records a linked correction (debit AR / credit the original bank account), and creates the unpaid subledger entry once. The original invoice number, original journal, revenue and tax remain intact. Correction dates must be in an open accounting period and not before the invoice date. This action is for erroneously recorded unpaid invoices, not refunds of actual customer payments.

Deploy migration `20261022_pos_ar_invoices` on EC2 and run `scripts/check_pos_ar_1022.py` (read-only). No historical invoice conversion or customer selection occurs in the migration. Existing source-controlled journal unpost safeguards remain in effect.
