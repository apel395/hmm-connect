# Reports and opening balances — 12 October

Profit and loss presents Pendapatan, Beban, HPP, operating salaries, operating expenses, GOI, overhead salaries, overhead expenses, GOP/GOL, under expense and NOP. Beban is a cost summary, not an additional deduction. Unmapped expenses are included in under expense so NOP includes every recorded expense. Category setup remains editable; no COA mapping is rewritten automatically.

Click a report section to inspect all related posting accounts, including zero balances and accounts hidden from the report's detail display setup. Account amounts link to GL with the selected period and organization scope. Expense contributions to profit subtotals appear negative. Screen and print show the summary rows; Excel retains detail and organizational sheets. The balance notice is removed from P&L screen and print.

Opening Balance Excel exports persisted snapshots for the selected year and authorized organization, preserving entered debit and credit separately. It includes eligible zero accounts and makes no journal, balance or database changes. Unsaved form edits must be saved to appear in the export.

Organization lookups and financial reports enforce assigned location/business unit and department roles. Legacy journal lines inherit business unit from their recorded location when the unit field is empty. Manual journal entries alone can be copied.

Fixed asset account choices use 100-600, accumulated depreciation uses 100-800, and prepaid 100-400 belongs to amortization. Asset posting validates the open accounting period and posts GL in the same transaction, carrying source organization on journal lines. Depreciation posting requires a single business unit/work location per run and preserves each asset's department.

No migration is required. Existing posted data is not rewritten.
